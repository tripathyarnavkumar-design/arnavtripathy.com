sections:
  - heading: The brief
    text: The project involved developing a cohesive visual communication system for
      Futuring Design Education 2024, a conference organised by the Department
      of Design, IIT Delhi, and IDC School of Design, IIT Bombay. The scope
      included designing conference posters, standees, large-format banners, and
      the Book of Abstracts. The visual identity used layered abstract forms, an
      eye motif, a light-blue colour palette, and consistent typography to
      establish a recognisable identity across print materials and event
      signage. The designs were adapted to different formats while maintaining
      visual consistency and ensuring that key information remained clear and
      accessible.
    prompt: What the conference needed from its visual materials.

  - heading: My contribution

      organising event information. I ensured consistency across the materials
      while adapting each composition to its specific format and communication
      requirements.
