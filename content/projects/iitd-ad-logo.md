  - heading: The idea behind the mark
    text: The logo is constructed from the letters I-I-T-D, stylised using visual
      characteristics of Arabic lettering. The letterforms are tilted and
      arranged to create a diamond-like silhouette, referencing the distinctive
      rooftop design of Dogra Hall at IIT Delhi. This approach brings together
      the institute’s architectural identity and the visual language of Arabic
      calligraphy, establishing a connection between IIT Delhi and its Abu Dhabi
      campus. The red colour is drawn from IIT Delhi’s existing logo,
      maintaining continuity with the parent institution’s visual identity.
